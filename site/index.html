A model server under your TV

It runs on the GPU

llama.cpp's Vulkan backend on the console's RDNA 2 GPU. Every architecture and quantization llama.cpp knows comes along.

It answers your network

OpenAI's /v1/chat/completions, Anthropic's /v1/messages (Claude Code works against it) and a chat page in any browser, at http://<console>:8081.

It decides, too

Load a decision model and the console answers /v1/systemone: choice, score and yes-or-no questions about text and images.

It shows its work

Every request streams across the TV as a chat: which device asked, what it asked, and the reply as it is written.

It watches the console

Charts of temperature, power and load, beside a table of every reading the console gives.

It picks what fits

The planner reads each GGUF and gives it the longest context and best cache that fit. Downloads go to internal storage, the M.2 drive or USB.

It reads images

Models with an image projector, such as Gemma 4, MiniCPM-V and d1, take pictures in chat and in decisions.

It needs nothing else

No PC while it runs, no cloud and no account. Your prompts never leave your home.

Powered by Ollaya

Decision models

Ollaya runs open decision models on a PC the way Ollama runs language models. PS5LM brings the same idea to the console: a decision model reads a state and answers typed questions in one forward pass, with calibrated probabilities and no text generated.

$ curl http://<console>:8081/v1/systemone -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{
  "state": "I was charged twice this month. Refund me or I cancel.",
  "questions": {
    "intent": {"type": "choice", "instructions": "What does the customer want?",
               "criteria": {"refund": null, "information": null, "cancel": null}},
    "angry":  {"type": "noul", "instructions": "Is the customer angry?"}
  }
}'

The Suggested list has d1 Omni 600M, d1 3B and Clef Flash. Any GGUF that carries llama.cpp's decision metadata loads the same way, and the app labels it a decision model.

Install

You need a PS5 on firmware 7.00 to 13.60, jailbroken, with these payloads loaded:

PayloadFor
kstuff-litethe kernel patches homebrew runs onrequired
etaHENhomebrew supportrequired
ShadowMountPlusputs PS5LM on the home screen from /data/homebrewrequired
elfldrtemperature, power and real free spacerecommended
ftpsrvinstalling the app, copying models from a PCrecommended
  1. Download ps5lm-app-vX.Y.Z.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Copy the PPSA99575 folder to /data/homebrew over FTP. ShadowMountPlus adds PS5LM to the home screen.
  3. Open PS5LM, go to Models > Suggested and get a model. Or copy a .gguf to /data/PS5LM/models, or to PS5LM/models on a USB drive.
  4. Load it. Once Home says Ready, open http://<console>:8081 in a browser, or point your OpenAI or Anthropic client at it.

To update, copy the new folder over the old one; settings, models and downloads stay in /data/PS5LM. Using it from coding tools (opencode, Codex, Claude Code) is in docs/CLI.md, building it in docs/BUILDING.md.

Questions

Does it run on the GPU?

Yes, every layer. llama.cpp's Vulkan backend runs on RADV, the open Mesa driver, ported to the PS5 by PS5_Vulkan. Generation is bound by memory bandwidth, and the 9B model reads its weights at about 70% of the GPU's 448 GB/s.

How big a model can it run?

About 11 GB of model and context. Qwen 3.8 27B at 2-bit fits with a 64k context; a 9B at 4-bit fits with room to spare. The planner tells you what fits before you download.

Does this run games or need any Sony code?

No. PS5LM runs open-weight models and nothing else. No games, keys, firmware or Sony SDK files are part of it.

Can it break my console?

It writes only to its own folders, never to system files. Under a long load the console runs warm and the fan spins up, as it does in a game; the Console page shows both.

Is it the same as PS5LM Plus?

PS5LM grew from PS5LM Plus, the native GPU app by abhi245y, and now lives under its own title ID, PPSA99575, so the two install side by side.
